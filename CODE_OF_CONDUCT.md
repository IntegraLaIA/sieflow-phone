# Code of conduct

SieFlow Phone follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/IntegraLaIA/sieflow-phone/security/advisories/new) or by contacting INTEGRALAIA. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
